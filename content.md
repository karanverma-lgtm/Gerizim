## Page 2: Company Review – About Us

### About Us
Gerizim is India’s leading Statutory Compliance Consultancy firm, serving top corporate houses, including multinational and transnational corporations.

We specialize in navigating complex legal frameworks to ensure businesses remain fully compliant, allowing them to focus on their core operations with peace of mind.

Business Process Outsourcing has drawn its focus in the current corporate world, wherein the companies are more focused towards the core operations and the non-core operations being outsourced to save cost, time and increase efficiency. Gerizim has the required infrastructure and the expertise to cater to the needs for the required sectors. As a part of the total business process solutions, Gerizim undertakes business-outsourcing activities viz.:

- Compliance of various statutory functions, obtaining licenses under Shops and Commercial Establishment Act, Contract Labour Act, PF Act, ESI Act, Building Construction Workers Act.
- Handling all returns under various acts, HRD activities.
- Initial setting up of the company, including the Registration of the company with the Registrar of Companies, obtaining appropriate licenses and other non-core business functions.
