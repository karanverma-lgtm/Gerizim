About Gerizim HR Solutions

Navigating complex legal frameworks so businesses remain 100% compliant while focusing on core operations with peace of mind.

Industry Leadership & Expertise

Gerizim is India’s leading Statutory Compliance Consultancy firm, serving top corporate houses, including multinational and transnational corporations across diverse sectors.

Business Process Outsourcing has drawn its focus in the current corporate world, wherein the companies are more focused towards the core operations and the non-core operations being outsourced to save cost, time and increase efficiency. Gerizim has the required infrastructure and the expertise to cater to the needs for the required sectors. As a part of the total business process solutions, Gerizim undertakes business-outsourcing activities viz.:

Full compliance of statutory functions under Shops Act, Contract Labour, PF, ESI & Construction Workers Act.
Filing returns under all labor statutes and managing complete HRD activities.
Initial setting up of the company, RoC registration, obtaining licenses and other non-core business functions.
